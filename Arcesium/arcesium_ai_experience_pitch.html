Internship / POC idea

From Black Box to Trusted Decisions

Designing AI experiences that increase confidence, adoption, and usage.

I’ve been exploring how Information Engineering + AI + human psychology can be combined to improve adoption of AI capabilities. I believe this could be especially relevant to platforms serving investment and asset-management users.

The idea in one sentence

Instead of asking users to trust a black-box model after it is built, first capture how they believe the world works, then let the system use data to validate, challenge, and extend that reasoning.

What the experience could feel like

Example: a portfolio manager is building a forecasting model.

👤
User
I expect TSMC to benefit when Apple demand strengthens because Apple is a major customer. I also think semiconductor demand and interest rates influence the forecast.
System
Got it. I’ll capture those as your hypotheses. Add any other relationships or market assumptions you believe matter.
🤖
👤
User
That captures the main drivers I have in mind.
System
Based on your hypotheses, I propose using: TSMC price and fundamentals, Apple demand/revenue indicators, semiconductor-sector data, interest rates and relevant market factors. Shall I use these data points for the analysis?
🤖
👤
User
Yes. Use those inputs.
System
Analysis complete. The Apple-demand relationship is visible in the data, while the interest-rate effect is weaker than expected. I also see a recurring movement not explained by your current hypotheses — here is a possible driver. Does it fit your understanding?
🤖

How it works

1

User describes the model in plain English

Capture the user’s hypotheses, relationships and domain intuition.

↓
2

System proposes the data it will use

Map the hypotheses to data already available on the platform and ask the user to confirm the inputs.

↓
3

System builds / analyzes the model

Run the analysis using the confirmed data and an appropriate modeling approach.

↓
4

AI compares findings with user reasoning

Show what is supported, unsupported, or not yet explained.

↓
5

User and system refine the reasoning together

The user can validate new patterns and evolve the model with the system.

Why this may matter

Understandable reasoning users already recognize
Trust-building AI validates and challenges assumptions
Higher adoption confidence can drive repeated use

This is broader than forecasting.

Forecasting is only one example. The same AI-Experience thinking could be explored differently for prediction, classification, GenAI and LLM-based workflows — with the goal of making AI easier to understand, trust and use.
